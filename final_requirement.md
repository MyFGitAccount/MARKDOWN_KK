### Front Page Requirements:
- Course: CCIT4080
- Class: CL15 | Group: 07
- Project Title: Educational Facilitation System
- Supervisor name
- All member names + 8-digit SIDs
- Semester: S2

### Logbook Entry Format – EACH ENTRY MUST HAVE:

| Field | Requirement |
|-------|-------------|
| **Date** | Exact date of entry (YYYY-MM-DD) |
| **Title/Activity** | Short title (e.g., "Meeting #4", "Sensor Calibration Test") |
| **Description** | 2-4 lines of what was done/found |
| **Milestone flag** | If applicable (e.g., "✅ Prototype v1 complete") |

### For Meeting Entries (Required):
- **Agenda** (bullet points of topics)
- **Brief minutes** (key discussion points)
- **Action items** with assigned members (format: `[Name] – Task – Deadline`)

### For Technical Decisions (Required):
- **Decision logged** (e.g., "Switched from HC-SR04 to VL53L0X distance sensor")
- **One-line reason** (e.g., "HC-SR04 had >20% error beyond 2m")
- **Trade-offs considered** (e.g., "VL53L0X cost 3x more but provides 1mm precision")
- **Rejected alternatives** (e.g., "Ultrasonic array rejected due to I2C address conflicts")

### Evidence Requirements:
- Screenshots with figure numbers
- Commit IDs (Git) with links
- Photos of hardware setup
- Test result tables/graphs

### What NOT to do:
- ❌ Daily diary of "what we did today"
- ❌ "Worked on code" (too vague)
- ✅ Systematic record of decisions, rationale, and reproducibility

### Intended Purpose Check:
> *"If any group member were required to redo the entire project alone in the future, the logbook + Final Report should provide enough information to reconstruct all essential steps, decisions, and justifications."*
