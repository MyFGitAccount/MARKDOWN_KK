## 4. Logbook (2nd Semester) (2.5%)

### Submission Details
| Item | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| **Submission platform** | SOUL |
| **Deadline** | 30 April 2026, 12:00 AM |
| **File format** | PDF |
| **File naming** | `CLXX_GroupYY_Logbook (2nd).pdf` (e.g., `CL30_Group01_Logbook (2nd).pdf`) |

### Content Requirements
Must include **1st semester logbook materials** as well (comprehensive record).

Should document:
- **Activities and Milestones** (chronological record)
- **Dates** for each activity/milestone
- **Descriptions** (tasks accomplished, findings, challenges, how difficulties were addressed)
- **Meeting Agenda and Minutes** (discussions, action items, decisions)
- **Key Decisions** (project direction, design choices, problem-solving strategies)

### Ideal Logbook Standards (Exceptionally Comprehensive)
Must include:
- All major decisions made throughout the project
- **Rationale** behind each decision (technical reasoning, constraints, trade-offs, rejected alternatives)
- Sufficient detail for **future reproducibility**

**Purpose:** If any group member had to redo the entire project alone in the future, the logbook + Final Report should provide enough information to reconstruct all essential steps, decisions, and justifications.

**Not** a diary of "what we did today" – rather a **systematic record of project thinking** including:
- Problem definitions and evolving understanding
- Task breakdown and planning
- Technical trials, experiments, and results
- Decision-making processes
- Issues encountered and how resolved
- Changes in design/approach (with reasons)
